## Description

```
People have been asking for a proper snorkel mod for years — and the couple that exist only fit one specific car. This is the first truly UNIVERSAL snorkel: real, visible snorkel parts on 27 vanilla vehicles, plus an invisible fallback system that protects literally ANY vehicle in the game, including mod cars.

Drive into a river. As long as the water stays below your snorkel opening, the engine keeps running. The moment the water reaches the opening — the engine starts flooding and hydrolocks, exactly like real life. Pick your snorkel for the depth you dare.

★ THREE SIZES ON EVERY SUPPORTED CAR
• 1. Small Snorkel — opening at ROOF height (~2 m). Classic safari look, wade up to your roofline.
• 2. Medium Snorkel — 3 m mast. Water over the roof? No problem.
• 3. Tall Snorkel — a 5 m mast for extreme crossings. Park on the bottom of a lake and keep idling.
All three are physical parts: black tube up the right A-pillar with an angled ram opening, mounting brackets, breakable in crashes (rip it off on a tree and deep water is your enemy again).

★ 27 SUPPORTED VEHICLES
D-Series, Roamer, Hopper, H-Series Van, Wydra, Grand Marshal, Moonhawk, Barstow, Bluebuck, Burnside, Miramar, LeGran, Lansdale, Wendover, Bastion, Covet, Pessima (old & new), Sunburst, Vivace, ETK 800 / I-Series / K-Series, Piccolina, Bolide, SBR4, Scintilla.
On the D-Series, Roamer, Van and Hopper the sizes appear inside the vehicle's own Snorkel slot, right next to the stock snorkel.

★ WORKS ON ANY OTHER CAR TOO (INCLUDING MODS)
The mod also includes an invisible "virtual snorkel" system for vehicles without a part: bind one key (Options > Controls > Vehicle > "Cycle snorkel mode") and cycle Off / Small (hood) / Medium (roof) / Tall (mast above roof) / Max (fully waterproof) on ANY vehicle. Your setting is remembered and applies to newly spawned vehicles automatically.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOW TO INSTALL
1. Click Subscribe / download the zip.
2. If downloaded manually: drop the zip into your mods folder (in game: Repository > Open mods folder, or Documents/AppData BeamNG.drive/<version>/mods). Do NOT unzip it.
3. Start the game (or enable the mod in the Mods menu).

HOW TO USE (visible snorkel)
1. Spawn a supported vehicle and open the Parts Selector (vehicle config).
2. D-Series / Roamer / Van / Hopper: open the vehicle's own "Snorkel" slot.
   All other supported cars: Additional Modification > "Snorkel (Right A-Pillar)".
3. Pick 1. Small (2 m), 2. Medium (3 m) or 3. Tall (5 m).
4. Go find deep water. Watch the opening — below it you're fine, at or above it your engine drowns.

HOW TO USE (any other car / mod cars)
1. Options > Controls > Vehicle > bind "Cycle snorkel mode".
2. Press it in any car: Off > Small > Medium > Tall > Max. On-screen messages show the active mode and warn you when your snorkel goes under.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NOTES
• Only the intake position matters for drowning in BeamNG — exhaust underwater is fine, and that's vanilla behaviour.
• An engine that already hydrolocked stays dead — the snorkel prevents damage, it doesn't repair it.
• Placement on some cars is still being fine-tuned — if a tube sits oddly on your favourite car, post a screenshot in the discussion and it'll get fixed.
• Fully open source, feedback and bug reports welcome!
```
